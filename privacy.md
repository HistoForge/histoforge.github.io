# Website privacy

This notice covers visits to the HistoForge website and documentation.

## Analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics to understand how people find and
use the website. It measures visits, page views, clicks on the HistoForge download
link and traffic sources, along with browser and device information and approximate
location. Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish visits.

Download-click totals can include repeated clicks and do not confirm that a file
finished downloading.

Analytics starts when you select **Allow analytics**. Your choice is saved in
your browser. Use **Analytics settings** in the footer to change it. Declining
stops analytics collection and removes this site's Google Analytics cookies.

Page addresses sent to Analytics omit query strings and gallery selections.
Referring addresses are limited to the referring website's origin. Advertising
personalization and Google signals are disabled.

Read [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
